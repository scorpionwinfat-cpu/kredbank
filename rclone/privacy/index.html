Privacy Policy — BFG rclone

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Purpose

BFG rclone is a private internal integration used by Baikal Finance Group to synchronize, back up and manage files between company-controlled computers and Google Drive through the open-source rclone software.

2. Google user data accessed

When authorized by the Google account owner, the integration may access Google Drive files, folders, file contents and file metadata that are necessary to perform requested file-management operations.

3. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to carry out file synchronization, backup, copying, listing, upload, download, update, deletion and related file-management operations explicitly initiated or configured by an authorized user.

4. Data storage and processing

The integration does not operate a separate public cloud service that collects or stores Google Drive data. File processing is performed by rclone on computers and systems controlled by Baikal Finance Group.

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens used by rclone are stored in the rclone configuration on systems controlled by Baikal Finance Group and are used only to authenticate requests to Google APIs.

5. Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their independent purposes. Data is transferred only as necessary between authorized systems and Google Drive through Google APIs.

6. Limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs is limited to providing and improving the functionality of this private integration and is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

7. Data retention

The integration itself does not maintain a separate database of Google user data. Local copies of files may exist on authorized systems as part of synchronization or backup operations configured by the user and are retained according to those local operational settings.

8. Access revocation

The Google account owner may revoke the application's access at any time through Google Account security settings. OAuth tokens can also be removed from the relevant rclone configuration.
