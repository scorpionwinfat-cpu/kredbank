BFG rclone — Google Drive Integration

BFG rclone is a private internal integration used by Baikal Finance Group to access, synchronize, back up and manage files between company-controlled computers and company-controlled Google Drive accounts using the open-source rclone software.

The integration is intended only for authorized internal use. It is not offered as a public service and is not intended for use by third parties.

Access to Google Drive is granted only after authorization by the relevant Google account owner and is used solely to perform file operations requested by that authorized user.
